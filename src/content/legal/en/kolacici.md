## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. In addition, the website may use your browser's local storage (localStorage) to remember settings.

## What this website uses

This website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `kr-consent` (localStorage) | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you delete it or change your choice |

## External content

Some parts of the website load content from other services, which may set their own cookies:

| Service | Where it is used | When it loads |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps | Map on the Contact page | Only if you accept external content |
| Google reCAPTCHA | Protects the contact and booking forms against spam | On pages with a form; required to send the form |

If you don't accept external content, you will see the address and a link to Google Maps instead of the map.

## How to change your choice

You can change your choice at any time via the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
